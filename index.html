Features

Everything you need, one overlay

Six battle-tested modules built specifically for Mistfall Hunter — each one toggleable in real time without restarting the game.

👁️

ESP Wallhack

See hunters, creatures and loot through walls with distance readouts, health bars and box outlines. Color-coded by threat so you always know what's coming.

🎯

Aimbot

Configurable target selection with FOV radius, smoothness and bone priority. Perfect for tracking fast-moving prey through the mist without snapping wildly.

🔫

No-Recoil

Eliminates vertical and horizontal kick on every weapon in the game. Your crosshair stays planted so follow-up shots land exactly where you aim.

⚡

Infinite Stamina

Never stop sprinting, dodging or climbing. Stamina drain is zeroed out, letting you chain evasions and outrun anything in the Mistfall wilds.

📦

Item Duper

Duplicate crafting materials and consumables straight from your inventory. Configurable stack multiplier keeps quantities believable so screenshots stay clean.

🛡️

God Mode Toggle

One key flips damage to zero while keeping hit reactions intact. Pair it with speed hack to move across the map at 2×–5× normal velocity.
